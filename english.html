About the City

Piconu City is a small city that grew up alongside the development of railway coupling technology. Its guiding idea is that "the parts that don't stand out are what support the whole" — a philosophy that has carried over from railway engineering into the city's approach to record-keeping, healthcare, and local government.

Things to See

Visitors are welcome to explore Piconu City on foot — most of the main sights are within walking distance of each other.

  • Nukapiko Folk Art Museum — local crafts and folk art
  • Kirokumachi Shopping Street — a small, traditional shopping district
  • The riverside promenade along the Nukapiko River
